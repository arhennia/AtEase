========================================================================
DEFINITION OF “PHASE 1 DONE”
========================================================================

A stranger with her WhatsApp link can:
  1. Open /p/[her-slug] on a phone
  2. Understand she comes to their home
  3. Pick a service with an “upto” price and a photo
  4. Book a time
  5. Type / pick their address
  6. Sign in with Google or phone
  7. See a confirmation

And she can:
  8. Open the dashboard on a phone
  9. See that booking
 10. Tap to call / WhatsApp the client
 11. Open Google Maps to their house

If those 11 things work, the website is real enough to test with her business.
Everything else is extra until then.
